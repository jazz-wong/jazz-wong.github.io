INERTIA

  Directed by Juan Diego Silva-Zúñiga, Inertia follows Damian (Maximilian Issacs) in an attempt to take his grandfather (Andrew Swanson) with dementia for a final ride before putting him to rest — though, he can’t seem to bring the day to an end.

Winner of “Best U.S Short Film” from the Brooklyn International Short Film Festival, “Official Selection” from the Downtown Film Festival, and winner of Fine Cuts Film Festival.

THE KING EDITION

A collection of the film’s spirit documented in a nice, glossy hardcover box-set.

  The Inertia Screenplay Book is an 8.5 x 11 inch hardcover book highlighting the film’s script and cinematography. It features character biographies, Silva-Zúñiga’s original storyboard sketches, and concept prop designs by Jazz Wong.

 The Inertia: Behind the Scenes is a 8.5 x 5 inch, screw binded zine that transforms the PA’s digital camcorder video into a physical documentation of the film’s production.

OFFICIAL POSTER

 The is an 27 x 40 inch, movie standard size poster. The main inspiration sprung from vernacular street imagery, like handicap parking and crosswalk lights, to emphasize the road trip aspect of the film. This icon also demonstrates the dependent relationship between the two protagonists, with Damian dragging Solomon through various adventures to celebrate his last day. Furthermore, the blood foreshadows the harrowing incident to come, tying together the themes of life and death.

MOVIE PROPS

 Featured at the climax of the short film, the design of Endwell’s cigarette packaging is inspired by Marlboros as a way to contrast the logo of an Ouroboros: a snake consuming its own tail in a circular manner. This symbol represents the continuous cycle of life, one of the main themes of Inertia. It stands tall at 3.35 x 2.24 x 0.87 inches.

SHAPESHIFTER LAB

  ShapeShifter Lab is a live music and arts event venue in Brooklyn specializing in jazz and blues. They masquerade in the day as a cafe, and transform into a bar at night.

MERCHANDISE

  A unique feature of Shapeshifter Lab is their live audio recording services, an overlooked attribute that has the potential to cultivate a new community. Inspired by Electric Lady Studios’ discography of live music sessions, Shapeshifter Lab collaborates with various local jazz artists to produce a compilation album. To promote the album, Shapeshifter Lab plays the music during cafe hours and sells the physical vinyl at the counter. It is a great way to bring the musical jazz community of Brooklyn together and raise up new voices.

SPARK (FOR KINGSTON)

  Directed by Carlos Key and Kalijah Rowe, Kingston is about a legendary Ivy League institution where three tales intertwine: a determined first-gen student seeks to resist her classmates’ elitism, a noncommittal situationship tests the fragility of Gen Z intimacy, and a tenured Chinese professor fights to innovate the language curriculum with the “help” of a delinquent legacy admit.

Winner of the 2026 Tribeca Festival in the U.S. Narrative Competition category.

Spark is the modern dating app used by the film's star-crossed situationship: Amber (Leann Gardner) and Marco (Nick Snipes).

NASA DISASTERS PROGRAM

  The NASA Disasters Program advances science and builds tools to help communities make informed decisions for disaster planning. By providing accessible satellite data and geospatial tools, the program reduces disaster impacts, supports response, and strengthens resilience alongside response partners and local networks.

Beatriz Lozano, LLC partnered with the NASA Disasters team to build an identity that communicates the science behind their work, the diversity of hazards they address, and the role communities can play in shaping their own resilience.

I had the pleasure to work with designers Michelle Castellanos and Emily Keren Li to photograph a series of personalized branding to use as mockups.

Jazz Wong is a chinese-filipina designer and freelance romantic, who creates for surreal narratives born in the vernacular world. She started her design career with KidPix on a 2000s Macintosh, and it was love at first sight. Since then, they’ve continued their path of being a graphic designer through learning the ins and outs of Photoshop, Illustrator, Indesign, and VS Code, depicting narrative expression through websites, booklets, posters, and photography.
